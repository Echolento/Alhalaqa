# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- **Primary**: Independent Quran teachers running their own halaqat (memorization circles). They manage students, schedule sessions, track memorization progress, and handle billing. They work from phones and laptops, often between lessons or at home.
- **Secondary**: Students of those teachers. They view their session schedule, check payment status, and see progress notes.
- **Tertiary**: Platform admins who manage organizations and oversee teachers.

## Product Purpose

Alhalaqa is a complete operational platform for independent Quran teachers. It replaces scattered WhatsApp messages, paper notes, and spreadsheets with a single hub for student management, attendance tracking, memorization progress, and billing. Success means a teacher can run their entire halaqa from this app without ever needing another tool.

## Brand Personality

grounded, purposeful, serene

The interface should feel like a tool made for serious work — not flashy, not gamified. The Quranic context demands dignity and restraint. Every element earns its place; nothing is decorative. The app is calm because the teacher's attention belongs to the student, not to the interface.

## Anti-references

- No generic v0/shadcn SaaS template look: no cream/sand backgrounds, no overly-rounded mega-cards, no standard shadcn sidebar with no personality, no generic dashboard card grid.
- No "religious software" aesthetic: no ornate borders, no decorative islamic geometric patterns, no green-dominant everything, no gold gradients.
- No gamified or playful design. This is a professional tool, not a classroom reward app.
- No heavy, dark, or moody interfaces. The feel should be airy and legible, not grim.

## Design Principles

1. **The teacher's focus is the student, not the UI.** The interface gets out of the way. High data density when needed, clear hierarchy always, zero unnecessary clicks.
2. **Restraint is respect.** No decoration that doesn't serve the task. No empty states that waste space. Every pixel has a job.
3. **Teal as the anchor, not the theme.** The existing primary teal-green is the accent; it marks interactivity and focus. The surfaces stay cool and neutral. Color is used sparingly to direct attention, not to decorate.
4. **Mobile as a first-class surface.** Teachers operate between sessions, on phones, in variable lighting. Touch targets, readability, and quick-glance information priority.
5. **Arabic first, RTL native.** The language is not an overlay; the layout is authored in RTL from the ground up.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast minimum (body text ≥4.5:1 against background)
- Mobile-first responsive design targeting phones as primary surface
- Touch targets ≥44px
- RTL support is not optional — it is the default orientation
- Reduced motion support for all animations
