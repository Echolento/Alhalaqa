## Overview
**Alhalaqa** is a focused payment-tracking platform for independent Quran teachers. It replaces scattered WhatsApp messages and paper notes with a simple monthly billing hub — track who has paid, who hasn't, and what to expect at a glance.

## Features
### Payment Management
Monthly billing per student with one-click toggle between paid/unpaid. Navigate between months and see collected vs. pending revenue at a glance.

### Student Management
Add, edit, bulk-add, and remove students. Each student has a name, phone, monthly fee, and billing day.

## Tech Stack
- **Framework:** [Next.js 16](https://nextjs.org/) (App Router & Server Actions)
- **Database:** [Supabase](https://supabase.com/) (PostgreSQL with Row Level Security)
- **Authentication:** [Supabase Auth](https://supabase.com/auth)
- **Styling:** [Tailwind CSS v4](https://tailwindcss.com/) & [Shadcn UI](https://ui.shadcn.com/)
- **Icons:** [Lucide React](https://lucide.dev/)
- **Testing:** [Vitest](https://vitest.dev/) (unit), [Playwright](https://playwright.dev/) (e2e)
- **Deployment:** [Vercel](https://vercel.com/)

## Localization
Built RTL-native with Arabic as the primary language. The interface is authored in Arabic from the ground up — no overlay, no left-to-right fallbacks.
