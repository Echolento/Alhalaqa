## ✨ Overview

**Alhalaqa** is a modern SaaS platform designed specifically for independent Quran teachers to streamline their administrative tasks. It provides a centralized hub to manage students, track progress, and handle financial aspects, allowing teachers to focus on what matters most: teaching.

> [!NOTE]
> **Live SaaS:** This platform is active and accessible at **[alhalaqa.com](https://alhalaqa.com)**.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 👥 Student Management
Maintain a clean, organized directory of all your students with their profiles, contact information, and enrollment status.

### 📅 Attendance Tracking
Log attendance for every session with a single click. View history to identify patterns and ensure consistency.

### 📖 Memorization Progress
Detailed tracking of Quranic memorization (Hifz) and revision. Monitor student milestones and current lesson progress.

### 💳 Payment & Revenue
Professional billing management. Track monthly fees, mark payments as received, and view your revenue dashboard at a glance.

### 💬 Frictionless Invitations
Invite new students via a dedicated WhatsApp-integrated flow, making onboarding quick and intuitive.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Framework:** [Next.js 15+](https://nextjs.org/) (App Router & Server Actions)
- **Database:** [Supabase](https://supabase.com/) (PostgreSQL with Row Level Security)
- **Authentication:** [Supabase Auth](https://supabase.com/auth)
- **Styling:** [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/) & [Shadcn UI](https://ui.shadcn.com/)
- **Icons:** [Lucide React](https://lucide.dev/)
- **Email:** [Resend](https://resend.com/)
- **Deployment:** [Vercel](https://vercel.com/)

---

## 🌍 Localization
The platform is built with native RTL (Right-to-Left) support and is primarily localized in **Arabic**, catering to the specific needs of Quranic education settings.
